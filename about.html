About Djalissa

We believe every child deserves the safest, most nurturing care. Djalissa connects trusted babysitters with loving families across Algeria.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide a bespoke childcare experience that adapts to your baby's individual world while setting the highest gold standard in safety and care.

🌟 Our Vision

A world where every parent can find peace of mind, and every child can thrive in a safe, loving environment.
